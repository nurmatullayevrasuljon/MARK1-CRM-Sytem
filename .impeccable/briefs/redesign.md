# Surface brief — MARK1 visual transformation

Scope: every shipped route (`/`, `/login`, `/signup`, `/privacy`, `/dashboard` and its six sections). Mode: mixed — Persuade on the landing page, Operate on the CRM.

Audience and job: a shop owner deciding whether MARK1 can be trusted with the business, and the same owner or a seller doing daily work inside it. The landing must produce a signup or login; the app must make a sale, a stock check and a debt payment fast and unambiguous.

Constraints: preserve all existing routes, `data-target` values, element IDs used by `script.js`, API contracts, permissions, calculations, the payment-id index, and the independent token architecture of `privacy.html`. Brand color `#4455EE` stays. No fabricated proof. No new dependencies.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A merchant's ledger rebuilt as software — precision, calm authority, and numbers you can trust. The surface refuses the generic admin template of six equal rounded KPI cards over a pastel dashboard, and refuses the marketing page of three identical feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Hairline-ruled surfaces on a paper-white or deep-navy ground; brand `#4455EE` as the single action color; tabular numerals; 11px tracked micro-labels used only as data labels; 10px card radius, 8px controls; two shadow levels at most, both with real offset. The signature is the ledger strip: metrics sit in one ruled container, divided by hairlines, with a single dominant figure — not six floating cards.

STORY: On the landing, the visitor sees the actual product, understands that sales, stock and debt are one connected record, and starts an account. In the app, the user reads the state of the business in one scan, then acts without hunting.

FIRST VIEWPORT (landing): a 12-column editorial grid. Left 6 columns: headline, one-sentence value line, two actions, a short trust line. Right 6 columns: a real, code-built dashboard frame showing the ledger strip, a revenue chart and a recent-sales list, labelled "Namuna ko'rinish". A thin capability rule sits directly under the hero.

FIRST VIEWPORT (app): 248px sidebar with grouped navigation and a strong active state; 64px top bar carrying page title and subtitle plus theme and profile; content begins with a ruled ledger strip, then a 2:1 chart row, then attention panels.

FORM: pinned by the brief — "MARK1 Premium Business OS"; no concept roll. Seed key: `brief-pinned/precision-ledger`.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
